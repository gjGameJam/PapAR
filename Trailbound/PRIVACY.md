# Trailbound Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 18, 2026

Trailbound is a spatial territory game for Snap Spectacles. It can be played offline against computer-controlled bots or, when you choose **JOIN SESSION**, with other players through Snap's Connected Lenses service.

## Offline play

When played offline, Trailbound does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Device tracking is processed locally only to place the arena and map walking movement to grid cells.

## Connected play

If you choose **JOIN SESSION**, the game uses Snap's Connected Lenses infrastructure to synchronize multiplayer state. This may include:

- Your Snap user ID and display name, used to assign a player slot.
- Grid-cell claim and trail ownership.
- Player grid positions, captures, deaths, and session presence.

That information is processed by Snap to provide the multiplayer session and is subject to Snap's Privacy Policy. Trailbound does not operate a developer-run server, analytics service, or advertising service.

The game does not request GPS or location permission and does not assign a custom `LocationAsset`. Colocated mapping is used only to align the shared game board for players in the same space.

## Audio

The game plays short built-in sound effects. It does not record microphone input.

## Children

Trailbound is intended for users aged 13 and older. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes and contact

Any future update to this policy will be included with the corresponding Lens release. Questions can be sent through the developer's public project issue tracker once that project is published.
